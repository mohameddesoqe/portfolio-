LiveLink

LiveLink is a digital platform designed to streamline job finding and management

LiveLink is a digital platform designed to streamline job finding and management by automating operational processes such as task assignments, attendance tracking, and supervision. The system consists of a mobile application (Android & iOS) for employees and supervisors, a web-based management portal, and a landing page for public information. Additionally, it integrates with SMS services for notifications and verification purposes.

Purpose LiveLink aims to facilitate job management by: 1- Providing job listings and application tracking for employees. 2- Allowing supervisors to assign tasks and oversee performance. 3- Enabling project managers to monitor project progress. 4- Granting system administrators control over platform operations. Core Components 1- Mobile Application (for Employees & Supervisors) 2- Web-Based Portal (for System Administration & Project Management) 3- Landing Page (for Public Awareness) 4- SMS Integration (for Notifications & OTP Verification)

Conclusion LiveLink is a robust job management platform with strong automation and tracking capabilities. By addressing usability enhancements and integrating AI-driven features, the platform can improve efficiency and engagement for job seekers, employees, and managers alike. Future developments should focus on better personalization, seamless integrations, and enhanced security to make LiveLink a leading solution in job management systems.
